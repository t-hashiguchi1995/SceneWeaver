# SceneWeaver Shisei-derived Pose Pack

Images are CC0 1.0 from Shisei v0.3.0 (https://github.com/wquguru/shisei).
SceneWeaver assigns its own IDs (`sw-pose-XXXX`) and labels. We do **not**
copy ai-pose-gallery pose numbers, names, or category taxonomy from Shisei NOTICE.

Selected line-art views only (mannequin / AI adults). Suggestive series excluded.

## Semantic QC

Pose labels/prompts must describe the **actual line-art bytes**.
Standing vs sitting mismatches are a pack FAIL even if `qc_status` was historically `pass`.
Pack remediated 2026-11-02 after A3 Human Review found seated images labeled standing.
